Data transparency

What the university can and cannot see

Included

Connection source, timestamps, current employer-reported stage, verified outcomes, response activity and aggregate demographic groupings.

Excluded

Private message content, recruiter notes, student private fields, employer payment information and job applications held in other systems.

Source labels

System-recorded connections, employer-reported stages and employer-verified outcomes are displayed separately.

Purpose: TapID measures the relationship created by an introduction. It does not replace career-fair registration, payment, job posting, application management, interview scheduling, or the university’s official graduate-outcomes process.
